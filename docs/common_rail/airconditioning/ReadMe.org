* Air Conditioning
  The A/C compressor control on the Audi 8P / CJAA-CBEA is different from older TDIs.

  The EDC17 does not directly drive the compressor. The PWM signal for the N280 regulator valve
  is generated by the climate control module (J255). Without that module the ECM cannot turn the
  compressor on by itself.

** Thoughts on Approach
   Keep the factory Toyota AC amplifier from the 1983 Pickup as the safety and enable controller.
   Have an external/custom controller generate the 500 Hz PWM that the N280 valve needs.

   - Toyota amplifier
     - Reads its original evaporator thermistor, pressure switches, blower interlock, and AC switch.
     - Its clutch-output wire is the master “compressor allowed” signal.
     - Under normal operation it should stay continuously on because the custom controller keeps the evaporator
       from reaching the icing point.
     - If something fails and the evaporator gets too cold, the amplifier opens its output and the
       custom controller drops PWM to 0.

   - Custom Controller
     - Has its own evaporator temperature sensor.
     - Runs a slow control loop that targets ~2–4 °C (just above freezing).
     - Outputs 500 Hz PWM to the N280 valve only while the Toyota amplifier clutch line is ON.
     - When the amplifier output goes low, PWM is forced to 0 immediately.

   Idle-up from the Toyota amplifier is not required. My guess is that the CBEA has enough low-rpm
   torque and the PWM compressor ramps in gradually. The amplifier’s RPM interlock (it waits for RPM
   to rise before enabling the clutch output) may need to be spoofed or bypassed so the clutch line
   can close or maybe the Toyota AC amplifier's idle adjustment knob can be turned down low enough.

*** CAN
    The climate request the EDC17 listens to is mClima_1 (0x5E0, 100 ms).  For basic operation the
    custom controller only needs to set the compressor-request bit and a plausible ambient
    temperature. Other signals (pressure, compressor load, blower load) can stay at 0.

    The ECM itself only broadcasts status bits (for example MO5_KlimaKompr in mMotor_5 / 0x480).
    It does not output a PWM command over CAN 🥲

    Theoretically!

*** Notes
    - N280 is a PWM-controlled regulator valve, not a simple clutch. Frequency is typically 500 Hz.
    - Minimum controllable duty cycle is usually around 30 %.
    - The Toyota amplifier’s evaporator thermistor remains connected so it can still act as a hard
      anti-icing backup.
    - Binary/Pressure switch stays in the amplifier circuit.
